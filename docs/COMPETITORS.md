## Zylo (USA)

Website: zylo.com
Founded: 2016, Indianapolis USA
Funding: $72.5M raised
Pricing: $2,000+/month, annual contract required

What they do well:
- Largest SaaS benchmark dataset — 40 million licenses, $40 billion in spend data
- Deep SSO integrations with Okta, Google, Azure AD
- Contract management with auto-renewal prevention
- Professional services team that negotiates SaaS contracts on your behalf
- AI features for report generation and contract metadata extraction

What they do poorly:
- Completely inaccessible to any company under 500 employees
- Priced in USD with no India presence or support
- No invoice parsing for shadow IT discovery
- Requires dedicated SaaS ops team to use effectively
- Long sales cycle — not self-serve

Where Spendix beats them:
- 1/40th the price in Indian rupees
- Self-serve onboarding, no sales call required
- Groq-powered invoice parsing for shadow IT (Zylo does not have this)
- Built for Indian market with Indian number formatting and Rs. currency
- Works for a 150-person company, not just Fortune 500

## Zluri (India — Bengaluru)

Website: zluri.com
Founded: 2020, Bengaluru
Funding: $34M raised (Series B)
Pricing: Custom enterprise pricing, minimum ~$500/month, targeting 1,000+ employees

What they do well:
- Built by Indians, understands Indian enterprise context
- Strong SSO integration layer
- Good application discovery via browser extension
- Access reviews and compliance workflows
- Reasonably well-designed product

What they do poorly:
- Pivoted fully upmarket after Series B — no longer serves SMEs
- Self-serve is gone — requires a sales demo
- Expensive for what smaller companies need
- Browser extension for discovery is invasive for employees
- No AI-powered invoice parsing

Where Spendix beats them:
- Serves the 100 to 1,000 employee segment Zluri abandoned
- No sales call required — sign up and start
- Groq invoice parsing vs. browser extension (less invasive)
- Significantly lower price point

## Torii (Israel / USA)

Website: torii.co
Founded: 2017, Tel Aviv
Funding: $50M raised
Pricing: $1,000+/month

What they do well:
- Strong application discovery via email scanning and SSO
- Clean, modern UI
- Good workflow automation (auto-deprovision ex-employees)
- Shadow IT discovery is their strongest feature

What they do poorly:
- No India market focus or support
- Priced in USD
- Complex to set up — requires significant integration work
- Overlap detection is limited
- No AI-generated recommendations

Where Spendix beats them:
- India-first pricing and support
- Groq AI recommendations vs. static rule-based suggestions
- Simpler setup — paste an invoice vs. complex email scanning setup
- Overlap detection with AI categorization
